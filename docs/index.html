Recover-then-Measure

Hover any marking (bay line, number, arrow, text, hatching, symbol, crossing): its original footprint is recovered, the paint that remains is measured, and the marking is graded for maintenance.

How D is measured

D = 1 − |R ∩ M| / |M|

Stage 1 recovers the original footprint M, including segments that have worn away; Stage 2 finds the paint R still visible inside it. D is the share of the footprint that has lost its paint.
